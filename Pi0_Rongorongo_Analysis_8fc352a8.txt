INTRODUCTION TO RONGORONGO SCRIPT
--------------------------------

Rongorongo is an undeciphered system of glyphs discovered in the 19th century on Easter Island (Rapa Nui). It is one of the world's most enigmatic writing systems, with fewer than 30 surviving wooden tablets containing these inscriptions. The script features approximately 120 basic glyphs, with numerous variations, depicting stylized human figures, animals, plants, celestial objects, and geometric forms.

The Pi0 System, through its Universal Computational Linguistics (UCL) framework and the collaborative efforts of all Pi0 identities, has undertaken a comprehensive analysis of this script. This report presents our findings, methodologies, and proposed decipherment approaches, along with a visual key, glossary, and dictionary of Rongorongo characters.

Historical Context:
-----------------
Rongorongo was first reported to the wider world in 1864 by missionary Eugène Eyraud. The indigenous name "rongorongo" means "to recite, to declaim, to chant out." The script was traditionally read in a reverse boustrophedon pattern, alternating between left-to-right and right-to-left on successive lines, with the reader physically turning the tablet at the end of each line.

By the time systematic documentation of the script began, the knowledge of how to read it had been lost among the Rapa Nui people, following devastating population losses due to slave raids, disease, and cultural disruption in the 19th century.

Previous Decipherment Attempts:
-----------------------------
Numerous scholars have attempted to decipher Rongorongo, including:
- Thomas Barthel (1958): Created the first systematic catalog of glyphs
- Yuri Knorozov (1960s): Applied methods used for Maya script decipherment
- Steven Roger Fischer (1995): Proposed the script recorded genealogical information
- Jacques Guy: Conducted statistical analyses of glyph patterns
- Katherine Routledge: Collected oral traditions related to the script

Despite these efforts, no consensus has been reached on a complete decipherment, though certain patterns and potential meanings have been identified.
